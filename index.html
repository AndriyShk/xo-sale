How the forecast works

We average two views of the real deposits: the sale keeps slowing down the way it has so far, or it keeps its recent pace. When deposits run far ahead of the slowdown, as in a last-day rush, only the recent pace counts, and in the last 6 hours that is the pace of the last hour. It can't see what nobody has announced yet: a big 1win promotion or a last-day rush. On the Jumper sale deposits grew 1.9× in the last 24 hours.

How the markets resolve

$10M: gross commitments as of Oct 30, 00:00 UTC, 16 hours before the sale ends. $20M: the gross total on the sale page at any time before the close (Oct 30, 16:00 UTC; an extension counts up to Nov 15). $30M: the same gross total at any time before Nov 3, 03:59 UTC. $50M: gross deposits by the close, as reported by ICO Drops, else Dealroom. All count deposits before refunds.
