How the model works

Sales raise fastest at the start and slow down later. The tracker fits deposits ∝ timek to the recorded deposits. With k = 1 money keeps coming at the same speed; with k = 0 it has stopped.

To reach $35M by the XO close, the sale needs k ≥ —. So far it's running at k ≈ —. The model's chance is how likely the real k is to stay above that line, given the uncertainty.

Not modelled: whales, last-minute rushes, Legion changing the sale. The signal is weak early on and gets stronger as data comes in.
