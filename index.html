How the forecast works

We average two views of the real deposits: the sale keeps slowing down the way it has so far, or it keeps the pace of the last 12 hours. The range around it stays wide, because a last-day rush or a whale can change a lot.

Two deadlines

Betting on XO closes Oct 1, 23:59 UTC. The Legion sale runs until Oct 2, 13:00 UTC. The rules don't say which one counts. We use the earlier one; with the later one the forecast is —.

Not advice

A whale or a last-minute rush can change everything. Only trade what you can afford to lose.
