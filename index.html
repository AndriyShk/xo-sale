Good to know

How the forecast works

We average two views of the real deposits: the sale keeps slowing down the way it has so far, or it keeps the pace of the last day. It can't see what nobody has announced yet: a big 1win promotion or a last-day rush. On the Jumper sale deposits grew 1.9× in the last 24 hours.

How the markets resolve

$10M: gross commitments as of Oct 30, 00:00 UTC, 16 hours before the sale ends. $20M: the gross total on the sale page at any time before the close (Oct 30, 16:00 UTC; an extension counts up to Nov 15). Both use the total before refunds, as shown on the sale page.
