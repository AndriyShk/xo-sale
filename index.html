Good to know

  • How the forecast works: token sales raise the most money in the first hours and slow down after. We measure that slowdown from the real deposits and extend it to the deadline. It gets more accurate every hour.
  • Two deadlines: betting on XO closes Oct 1, 23:59 UTC, but the Legion sale runs until Oct 2, 13:00 UTC. The rules don't say which one counts. The forecast uses the earlier one; with the later one it would be —.
  • Not advice: a whale or a last-minute rush can change everything. Trade only what you can afford to lose.
