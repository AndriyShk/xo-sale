How the model works

Token sales raise fastest at the start and slow down later. The tracker fits a simple curve, deposits ∝ timek, to the deposits recorded so far. A k near 1 means money keeps coming in at the same speed; near 0 means it has stopped.

To get from today's total to $35M before the XO market closes, the sale needs k ≥ —. So far it's running at k ≈ —. The chance is how often that holds once the model's uncertainty is included.

Not modelled: big whales, last-minute rushes, and Legion changing the sale. This is a rough guide, not financial advice.

How Jumper compares with past Legion sales (total deposited)

Resolution rules (from XO)

YES if total deposits for the Jumper sale on Legion.cc reach 35,000,000. Primary source: the Legion invest page. If the sale is cancelled before the cutoff, or the data becomes permanently unavailable, the market voids and every position resolves equally.

⚠️ The XO market closes on Oct 1, 23:59 UTC, but the Legion sale runs until Oct 2, 13:00 UTC. The rules don't say which moment counts. The tracker shows both; ask the creator or XO if unsure.
